Connect your console

Xbox setup ＋
  1. On your Xbox, open Settings → Devices & connections → Remote features and enable remote features.
  2. Open Xbox Remote Play, sign in with the Microsoft account linked to your Xbox, and select your console.
  3. Click Capture inputs to play. Press Esc to return to the cursor.
PS5 setup ＋

Enable Settings → System → Remote Play → Enable Remote Play on your PS5. Open Docker Desktop on this computer, choose Start PS5 Player, then Open PS5 Remote Play to pair your console.

Play in Google Chrome ＋

Use this option if sign-in or streaming works better in Chrome.

Click Set up Chrome to copy the extension folder path and open Extensions. Turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Press ⌘⇧G, paste the path, then select the folder.

Already installed? Click Set up Chrome, click the reload arrow on the Remote Pad extension, then refresh the Remote Play tab.
