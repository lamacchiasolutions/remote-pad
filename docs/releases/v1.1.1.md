Remote Pad 1.1.1 lets you play your Xbox console from a Mac using a keyboard and mouse as an Xbox-style controller. The same app runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later.

**Download `Remote-Pad-macOS.zip` from Assets**, unzip it, and drag **Remote Pad.app** to Applications. The download is ad hoc signed and has no Apple notarization. Review the installation instructions for the first launch. [Installation guide](https://github.com/lamacchiasolutions/remote-pad#install)

- Turn with the mouse while aiming and firing independently. Adjust sensitivity and minimum look strength to suit the game.
- Select buttons, triggers and stick directions on the 3D controller to change their assignments.
- Choose **Test inputs** to check your layout before connecting to your console. An optional Chrome extension is included.

The packaged app passed the input, saved-layout and signed-out Xbox player checks. Testing includes fullscreen mouse look and simultaneous trigger presses. A signed-in gameplay session has not been independently verified for this release.
